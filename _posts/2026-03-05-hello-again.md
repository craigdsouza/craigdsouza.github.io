This site has been dormant for a few years. I'm bringing it back — not as an archive, but as a working space.

The old posts are still in the repository but unpublished for now. Some of them hold up; others feel like artifacts from a different moment. I'll decide what to resurface, if anything, as I go.

**What this space is for:**

*Writings* will be shorter pieces — things I'm thinking through, processes I've figured out, ideas I want to test by putting them into words.

*Reports* will be longer, more structured. I've been experimenting with AI-assisted research and want to document what that produces when done carefully.

*Tools* will be small utilities I build that might be useful to others — nothing grand, just things that solve real problems.

**What I'm working with:**

Data, mostly. I've spent time in the water sector, open data, GIS, and more recently AI tools applied to research contexts in India. I'm interested in what it looks like to do rigorous technical work when the institutions and resources around you are thin.

If something here is useful or you want to connect — my email is on the [About](/about) page.

More soon.
