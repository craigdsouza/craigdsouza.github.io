# 
ChatGPT recently announced a [Plaid Connector](https://www.livemint.com/technology/tech-news/you-can-now-connect-bank-accounts-track-spending-and-ask-personal-finance-questions-to-chatgpt/amp-11778893110633.html?utm_campaign=fullarticle&utm_medium=referral&utm_source=inshorts). This means users in the U.S. can directly ask chatGPT about their bank transactions. chatGPT, via Plaid (a data aggregator ) can obtain user's financial data with their permission and can help users plan their budgets, spend wisely and increase savings. Solo builders on Replit also learned of the announcement of a similar [feature](https://plaid.com/blog/build-personalized-finance-apps-replit-plaid/) in April of 2026. In fact Plaid connectors are making their way
