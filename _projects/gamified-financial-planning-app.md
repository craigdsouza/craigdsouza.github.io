# A Money Game - Duolingo for Financial Planning

A full fledged android app - the idea was "Duolingo like gamification for financial planning". The app has a full set of features including 

![amoneygame](/img/tools/amoneygame.png)

0) bite sized lessons to learn about money  
1) expense creation via a one click entry, edit and delete expenses.  
2) categorization , including custom category creation  
3) multiple views - table, calendar, budget card for comprehensive tracking  
3) full offline functionality, including remote sync when back online  
4) family plan for family expense tracking  
5) gamification to increase likelihood of expense tracking  
6) basic animations to increase engagement.
